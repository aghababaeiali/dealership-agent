# Northgate Motors Returns Policy

*Effective January 15, 2024*

> **This document has been superseded by the returns policy effective
> June 1, 2026. It is retained here for historical reference only and
> does not reflect current terms.**

## The 7-Day / 500-Mile Return Window

Northgate Motors offers a **7-calendar-day, 500-mile "Buy Back Guarantee"**
on every used vehicle we sell, no questions asked. The window begins at
the moment you sign the delivery paperwork and take possession of the
vehicle. Both limits apply simultaneously: if you drive more than 500
miles, the return window closes immediately even if fewer than 7 days
have passed.

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a return under the Buy Back Guarantee, the vehicle must:

- Have no new damage beyond normal wear since delivery.
- Be returned with all original accessories: both key fobs, floor mats,
  and the owner's manual.
- Not have any liens, tickets, or accidents reported against it during
  your possession.

## What You Get Back

If your return is approved, Northgate Motors refunds 100% of your down
payment and cancels the purchase contract in full. The $399 documentation
fee in effect at the time is non-refundable. Any trade-in vehicle
surrendered as part of the deal will be returned to you in its original
condition if still on our lot.

## Vehicles Not Eligible for Return

The Buy Back Guarantee does not apply to vehicles sold "as-is" at our
wholesale/auction lot location, vehicles with a purchase price under
$2,000, or fleet purchases of 3 or more vehicles in a single transaction.

## How to Initiate a Return

Call our sales office and speak with a manager, then bring the vehicle,
both keys, and all original paperwork to the dealership during business
hours. A sales manager will inspect the vehicle and mileage on the spot.
If approved, refund processing begins the same day.

## One Return Per Household

Northgate Motors limits the Buy Back Guarantee to one return per
household per rolling 12-month period.
