# Northgate Motors Trade-In Policy

*Effective March 1, 2026*

## Getting an Offer

Northgate Motors provides trade-in offers in two ways: an instant online
estimate (based on your vehicle's year, make, model, mileage, and
condition self-reported through our website), and a firm written offer
following an in-person appraisal. Only the in-person appraisal produces a
binding offer - online estimates typically run 5-15% higher than the
final in-person offer once actual condition is assessed.

## The Appraisal Process

An in-person appraisal takes approximately 30-45 minutes and includes:

- A test drive of up to 5 miles by a certified appraiser
- A visual inspection of exterior body panels, glass, and tires
- An OBD-II diagnostic scan for stored trouble codes
- A CARFAX or AutoCheck history report pull (free, no obligation)
- Verification of title status, lien payoff (if applicable), and odometer
  reading against the title

You do not need an appointment for an appraisal, but scheduling one in
advance (available same-day in most cases) reduces your wait to under
10 minutes.

## Written Offer Validity

A written trade-in offer is valid for **7 calendar days** from the date of
appraisal, provided the vehicle's condition, mileage, and title status
remain unchanged. If you drive the vehicle more than 500 additional miles
before completing the deal, we reserve the right to re-appraise.

## Outstanding Loans and Negative Equity

If you still owe money on your trade-in, we will pay off your existing
loan directly to your lender as part of the transaction. If your loan
balance exceeds the trade-in offer (negative equity), the difference is
either rolled into your new financing (subject to lender approval and
loan-to-value limits) or paid by you at signing. We disclose the exact
negative equity amount, in writing, before you sign any purchase
agreement.

## Trade-In Without a Purchase

You may accept a written trade-in offer and sell us your vehicle even if
you do not buy a car from us that day. In this case, payment is issued by
check within 3 business days rather than same-day, since same-day cash
payment is reserved for vehicles traded in as part of an active purchase.

## Vehicles We Cannot Accept

Northgate Motors cannot accept trade-ins with:

- A salvage, flood, or "junk" title, unless separately arranged through
  our wholesale partner (offers for these are typically 40-60% below
  clean-title market value)
- Unresolved liens where the payoff amount cannot be verified within 24
  hours
- Open manufacturer safety recalls affecting drivability (we can still
  appraise the vehicle once the recall is completed at no cost to you at
  a franchise dealer)

## Multiple Trade-Ins

We accept up to 2 trade-in vehicles per single purchase transaction.
Combined trade-in equity from both vehicles may be applied toward your
down payment or new vehicle purchase price.

## Sales Tax Credit

In states that allow a trade-in sales tax credit (reducing the taxable
amount of your new purchase by your trade-in value), Northgate Motors
applies this credit automatically at signing. States without this credit
are listed in our Fees and Taxes policy. This credit only applies when the
trade-in and new purchase are part of the same transaction.
