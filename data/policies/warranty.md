# Northgate Motors Warranty Policy

*Effective March 1, 2026*

## Overview

Every vehicle sold by Northgate Motors includes warranty coverage. The
coverage tier depends on whether the vehicle carries our Certified
Pre-Owned (CPO) designation or is sold as a standard used vehicle. This
document describes both tiers, what they cover, and how to file a claim.

## Standard Used Vehicle Limited Warranty

All non-CPO used vehicles include a complimentary **90-day / 4,000-mile
limited powertrain warranty**, whichever comes first, starting on the
delivery date. Powertrain coverage includes the engine block and internal
components, transmission and transfer case, and front/rear drive axles.
It does not cover wear items (brake pads, wiper blades, belts, hoses,
tires, batteries) or damage from accidents, misuse, or lack of
maintenance.

Repairs under this warranty must be performed at a Northgate Motors
service center or a partner shop pre-authorized by our service department.
Unauthorized repairs are not reimbursed.

## Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) Warranty

Vehicles that pass our 150-point CPO inspection (model year 6 years or
newer, under 80,000 miles at time of sale) are eligible for CPO status
and receive two layers of coverage:

- **7-year / 100,000-mile limited powertrain warranty**, calculated from
  the vehicle's original in-service date (not the resale date). Deductible
  is $100 per repair visit.
- **12-month / 12,000-mile comprehensive warranty**, calculated from the
  resale date, covering electrical systems, air conditioning and heating,
  steering and suspension, and fuel systems in addition to powertrain.
  Deductible is $50 per repair visit.

CPO vehicles also include 24/7 roadside assistance (towing, flat tire
change, lockout service, and fuel delivery) for the duration of the
12-month comprehensive term, and up to 5 days of rental car reimbursement
at $35/day if a covered repair keeps your vehicle in the shop overnight.

## Extended Service Contracts

For vehicles outside CPO eligibility, or for CPO buyers who want coverage
beyond the included terms, Northgate Motors offers optional Vehicle
Service Contracts (VSCs) underwritten by a third-party provider. VSC terms
range from 12 months/12,000 miles up to 60 months/75,000 miles, priced
between $1,295 and $2,895 depending on term length and vehicle
age/mileage at purchase. VSCs must be purchased within 30 days of the
vehicle sale date; they cannot be added later. See our Fees and Taxes
document for VSC line-item pricing at signing.

## Filing a Claim

1. Contact the Northgate Motors service department at least 24 hours
   before bringing the vehicle in, except in the case of a breakdown
   requiring roadside assistance.
2. Bring your original bill of sale and warranty documentation.
3. A service advisor will open a claim and provide a diagnostic estimate
   before any repair work begins under warranty.
4. Approved repairs are completed on-site whenever parts are in stock;
   otherwise expect 3-5 business days for parts to arrive.

## Exclusions

This warranty does not cover: pre-existing conditions disclosed at time
of sale, damage from flooding or collision, aftermarket modifications,
commercial or rideshare use (unless the vehicle was purchased for that
purpose and disclosed as such), or vehicles that have missed two or more
consecutive scheduled maintenance intervals as outlined in our Service and
Maintenance policy.

## Transferability

The Standard Used Vehicle Limited Warranty and the CPO Comprehensive
Warranty are not transferable to a subsequent owner if you resell the
vehicle. The CPO 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain warranty is transferable
one time for a $75 transfer fee, provided the transfer is processed within
30 days of the private-party resale.
