# Northgate Motors Returns Policy

*Effective June 1, 2026 - supersedes all prior versions of this policy*

## The 5-Day / 250-Mile Return Window

Northgate Motors offers a **5-calendar-day, 250-mile "Buy Back Guarantee"**
on every used vehicle we sell, no questions asked. The window begins at
the moment you sign the delivery paperwork and take possession of the
vehicle. Both limits apply simultaneously: if you drive more than 250
miles, the return window closes immediately even if fewer than 5 days
have passed.

This is a change from our previous 7-day/500-mile policy (see the
superseded version of this document for historical reference); the
current 5-day/250-mile terms apply to all vehicles purchased on or after
June 1, 2026.

## Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for a return under the Buy Back Guarantee, the vehicle must:

- Have no new damage beyond normal wear since delivery (dings, scratches,
  interior stains, or odor beyond what existed at time of sale are
  assessed by our reconditioning team and may result in a deduction).
- Be returned with all original accessories: both key fobs, floor mats,
  owner's manual, and any spare parts provided at delivery.
- Not have any liens, tickets, or accidents reported against it during
  your possession.
- Have all financing paperwork still within the 3-day rescission window
  if financed through a third-party lender (see Financing policy) - if
  your loan has already funded, a return still cancels the sale but may
  take 5-7 business days for the lender to process the loan reversal.

## What You Get Back

If your return is approved, Northgate Motors refunds 100% of your down
payment and cancels the purchase contract in full. The $499 documentation
fee is non-refundable once title paperwork has been filed with the state,
which typically happens within 48 hours of sale - returns processed within
that 48-hour window receive the full doc fee back as well. Any trade-in
vehicle surrendered as part of the deal will be returned to you in its
original condition if still on our lot; if it has already been sold or
sent to auction, we will refund the agreed trade-in value in cash or
check.

## Vehicles Not Eligible for Return

The Buy Back Guarantee does not apply to:

- Vehicles sold "as-is" at our wholesale/auction lot location (clearly
  marked at time of sale with a separate as-is disclosure form).
- Vehicles with a purchase price under $3,000.
- Fleet or commercial purchases of 3 or more vehicles in a single
  transaction.
- Vehicles that have been in an accident, received a salvage or rebuilt
  title designation, or been reported stolen during the return window.

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Call our sales office and speak with a manager - returns cannot be
   initiated by email or online form.
2. Bring the vehicle, both keys, and all original paperwork to the
   dealership during business hours (Monday-Saturday, 9am-7pm; closed
   Sunday).
3. A sales manager and a reconditioning technician will jointly inspect
   the vehicle and mileage on the spot.
4. If approved, refund processing begins the same day. Down payments made
   by card are refunded to the original card within 5-7 business days;
   cash or check down payments are refunded by check within 10 business
   days.

## One Return Per Household

Northgate Motors limits the Buy Back Guarantee to one return per
household per rolling 12-month period, to prevent the program from being
used as a short-term rental arrangement. A household is defined as
individuals sharing a billing address.
