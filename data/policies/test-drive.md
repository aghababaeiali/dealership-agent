# Northgate Motors Test Drive Policy

*Effective March 1, 2026*

## Eligibility Requirements

To test drive any vehicle at Northgate Motors, the driver must:

- Be at least **21 years old**
- Present a valid, unexpired driver's license (a temporary/paper license
  is accepted only if accompanied by a physical or digital copy of the
  full license record)
- Provide proof of active auto insurance, or sign our short-term test
  drive insurance waiver, which extends limited liability coverage
  through our garage policy for the duration of the drive
- Have no more than 2 moving violations or at-fault accidents on record in
  the past 3 years, per a quick check we run through our insurance
  provider's verification tool (this does not affect your personal credit
  or insurance rating)

We photograph the front of your license and keep it on file only for the
duration of your visit; it is not retained after you leave unless you
proceed with a purchase, in which case it becomes part of your deal
file per standard recordkeeping.

## Duration and Route

Standard test drives last up to **30 minutes** and cover a **15-mile**
pre-approved route that includes a mix of surface streets and highway
driving, so you can evaluate the vehicle at different speeds. Longer test
drives (up to 90 minutes) are available by appointment for customers who
are actively comparing 2 or more vehicles and want to drive them back to
back; ask your sales associate to arrange this in advance.

## Accompaniment

A sales associate accompanies every test drive. This is a company policy
applied uniformly and is not a reflection of trust in any individual
customer - it also allows the associate to answer questions about the
vehicle's features in real time. One additional passenger from your
household is permitted; additional passengers require manager approval.

## Extended and Overnight Test Drives

For certain higher-value vehicles (typically $40,000+), we may offer an
**overnight test drive** for serious buyers, subject to manager approval,
a completed credit pre-qualification, and a $500 refundable hold on a
credit card. Overnight test drives are limited to 24 hours and 75 miles
and must be returned with a full tank of fuel.

## Multi-Vehicle Test Drives

You may test drive as many vehicles as you'd like in a single visit, though
we ask that you allow about 20 minutes between drives for our team to
prep the next vehicle (checking fluid levels, tire pressure, and cleaning
as needed).

## What Happens If the Vehicle Is Damaged

Damage that occurs during a standard, accompanied test drive due to normal
driving is covered by our garage liability policy at no cost to you. If an
accident occurs due to reckless driving, driving under the influence, or a
violation of the approved route, you may be held financially responsible
for damages up to our insurance deductible ($1,000), and the incident will
be reported to the appropriate authorities as required by law.

## Test Drives for Out-of-State Buyers

If you are traveling from out of state specifically to test drive and
potentially purchase a vehicle, call ahead so we can confirm the vehicle
is on-site (not in transit between lots) and have it pulled up and ready
before you arrive. We're happy to do a video walk-around call in advance
for out-of-state buyers, but a live test drive still requires an in-person
visit - we do not permit remote or self-guided test drives under any
circumstances.
