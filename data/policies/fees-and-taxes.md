# Northgate Motors Fees and Taxes Policy

*Effective March 1, 2026*

## Documentation Fee

Every purchase includes a **$499 documentation ("doc") fee**, which covers
title work, lien filing (if financed), and administrative processing of
your sale. This fee is disclosed on the initial price quote and is the
same regardless of payment method (cash, finance, or lease). It is
non-negotiable and set dealership-wide, not per salesperson.

## Title, Registration, and License Fees

Title, registration, and license plate fees are set by your state of
residence, not by Northgate Motors, and are passed through at cost.
Typical ranges we see across the states we serve:

| Item | Typical Range |
| --- | --- |
| Title transfer fee | $15 - $95 |
| Registration/plate fee | $50 - $250 |
| Lien filing fee (financed purchases) | $10 - $35 |

We collect these fees at signing and remit them to your state's
Department of Motor Vehicles (or equivalent agency) on your behalf,
typically within 30 days of sale.

## Sales Tax

Sales tax is calculated based on your state and county of residence (where
the vehicle will be registered), not the location of our dealership.
Rates vary from 0% (in states with no sales tax on vehicle purchases) to
over 9% in some counties. If your state offers a trade-in tax credit
(reducing the taxable amount by your trade-in value), it is applied
automatically - see our Trade-In policy for details. We calculate your
exact tax amount during the paperwork process and disclose it in writing
before you sign.

## Emissions and Safety Inspection Fees

Some states require an emissions test or safety inspection before a
vehicle can be titled and registered. Where applicable, Northgate Motors
performs this inspection prior to delivery and passes through the actual
state-mandated fee, typically **$25-$45**. If a vehicle fails inspection,
we perform necessary repairs at no charge to you before delivery.

## Optional Add-On Products

The following are optional and never bundled into your price without
explicit written consent:

| Product | Price Range |
| --- | --- |
| Vehicle Service Contract (extended warranty) | $1,295 - $2,895 |
| GAP insurance (covers loan/value gap if totaled) | $695 - $895 |
| Paint and fabric protection package | $395 - $695 |
| Tire and wheel road hazard protection | $495 - $795 |
| Key replacement protection | $195 - $295 |

Each add-on is itemized separately on your purchase contract with its own
line item and price, and you may decline any or all of them without
affecting your approval for financing or the vehicle's sale price.

## Dealer Advertising and Market Adjustment Fees

Northgate Motors does not charge market adjustment fees, addendum
stickers, or dealer advertising fees above the advertised price. The
price you see listed online, less any applicable discounts or trade-in
credit, plus the doc fee and government fees/taxes described above, is
the total out-the-door price.

## Fee Disclosure Timing

All fees are disclosed in an itemized worksheet before you sign a
purchase agreement, and again on the final bill of sale. If any fee on
your final paperwork differs from the itemized worksheet you were shown,
you are entitled to a full explanation before signing, and may cancel the
transaction without penalty if the discrepancy is not resolved to your
satisfaction.
