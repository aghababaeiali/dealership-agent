# Northgate Motors Financing Policy

*Effective March 1, 2026*

## Overview

Northgate Motors arranges financing through a network of 9 partner
lenders, including regional credit unions and national auto-finance
companies. We do not lend money directly; every loan is originated and
funded by one of our partner lenders, with Northgate Motors acting as the
dealer of record.

## Credit Tiers and APR Ranges

Approved APR depends on your credit tier as reported by our lending
partners at time of application:

| Tier | Credit Score Range | Typical APR Range | Max Term |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 (Super Prime) | 720+ | 5.99% - 8.49% | 72 months |
| Tier 2 (Prime) | 660-719 | 8.50% - 12.99% | 72 months |
| Tier 3 (Near-Prime) | 600-659 | 13.00% - 17.99% | 60 months |
| Tier 4 (Subprime) | 550-599 | 18.00% - 21.99% | 48 months |

Rates listed are illustrative ranges based on recent approvals and are not
guaranteed; your actual APR is set by the approving lender based on the
full application, including income, debt-to-income ratio, and the
specific vehicle being financed.

## Down Payment Requirements

A minimum down payment of **10% of the purchase price or $500, whichever
is greater**, is required for all financed purchases. Tier 3 and Tier 4
applicants may be asked for a larger down payment (typically 15-20%) at
the lender's discretion. Down payments may be made by cash, debit card,
cashier's check, or approved trade-in equity - personal checks are not
accepted for down payments over $1,000.

## Application and Approval Timeline

Most applications receive a preliminary decision within 2 hours during
business hours, and final approval, pending income/employment
verification, within 24 hours. Pre-qualification (a soft credit pull that
does not affect your credit score) is available online and gives you an
estimated rate range before you visit the dealership. A formal application
requires a hard credit inquiry.

## Co-Signers and Joint Applications

We accept co-signers and joint applicants. A co-signer must provide the
same documentation as the primary applicant (proof of income, government
ID, proof of residence) and will be equally liable for the loan. Adding a
qualified co-signer can move an applicant from Tier 3 or 4 into Tier 2
pricing in many cases.

## Required Documentation

- Valid driver's license or state-issued photo ID
- Proof of income (2 most recent pay stubs, or 2 years of tax returns if
  self-employed)
- Proof of residence (utility bill or lease dated within 60 days)
- Proof of auto insurance meeting your state's minimum liability
  requirements before the vehicle can be delivered

## Early Payoff and Prepayment

None of Northgate Motors' financing partners charge a prepayment penalty.
You may pay off your loan in full at any time without additional fees
beyond the interest already accrued.

## 3-Day Right to Rescind

For financed purchases, you have 3 business days from the contract
signing date to rescind the financing agreement without penalty, provided
the vehicle is returned in its original condition with no more than 250
miles added. This is separate from, and runs concurrently with, the
Buy Back Guarantee described in our Returns policy.

## Declined Applications

If your financing application is declined by all partner lenders, your
deposit (if any) is fully refunded within 3 business days, and the
purchase contract is void. We will offer to discuss alternative vehicles
in a lower price range that may fit an adjusted budget.
