# Northgate Motors Delivery Policy

*Effective March 1, 2026*

## In-Store Pickup

Most customers complete their purchase and drive the vehicle home directly
from the dealership the same day, once financing (if applicable),
insurance verification, and paperwork are complete. Same-day pickup is
available until 6pm on weekdays and 5pm on Saturdays to allow time for
final inspection and fuel top-off. We are closed Sundays.

## Local Home or Office Delivery

We offer free local delivery within a **25-mile radius** of our
dealership. Beyond 25 miles, delivery is billed at **$2.50 per mile**
each way, calculated from our dealership address, up to a maximum service
radius of 150 miles. Local delivery is scheduled within **3 business
days** of finalized paperwork and typically arrives within a 2-hour
window that we confirm by phone or text the day before.

## What Happens at Delivery

Our delivery driver will:

- Walk through the vehicle's features with you (or the receiving party,
  if different from the buyer, provided ID matching the purchase
  paperwork is presented)
- Confirm both key fobs are present and functioning
- Collect any remaining balance due, if full payment was not completed at
  signing (cashier's check or verified electronic payment only - delivery
  drivers do not accept cash)
- Provide a delivery receipt that starts your Buy Back Guarantee window
  (see Returns policy) at the moment of signature, not at the time your
  purchase paperwork was originally signed at the dealership

## Out-of-State and Long-Distance Delivery

For delivery beyond our 150-mile local radius, we arrange transport
through a licensed third-party auto shipping carrier. Estimated cost
ranges from $0.60-$1.10 per mile depending on distance, route, and open
vs. enclosed transport, paid directly to the carrier. Northgate Motors
coordinates scheduling but is not responsible for delays caused by the
carrier, weather, or road conditions. Typical transit time is 3-7 business
days for routes within 1,000 miles.

Because the Buy Back Guarantee's mileage limit is calculated from vehicle
delivery, and long-distance transport does not add odometer miles, the
return window for carrier-delivered vehicles begins when the carrier
signs the vehicle over to you, confirmed by the bill of lading.

## Delivery Scheduling Windows

We deliver Monday through Saturday, 9am-6pm. We do not offer evening
(after 6pm) or Sunday delivery at this time. If no one is available to
receive the vehicle at the scheduled window, we will attempt redelivery
the next business day; a $75 missed-delivery fee applies after the first
missed appointment.

## Delivery Vehicle Condition Guarantee

Every delivered vehicle is guaranteed to arrive with a full tank of fuel
(or a full charge for EVs, defined as 90%+ state of charge), all fluids
topped off, and a completed pre-delivery inspection checklist, a copy of
which is provided with your paperwork. Any damage that occurs during
transport before delivery is Northgate Motors' responsibility to repair
or replace at no cost to you, provided it is noted on the delivery receipt
at the time of handoff.

## Delivery to a Different Address Than Billing

We can deliver to an address other than your billing address (for
example, a workplace) as long as it is within our service radius and you
provide advance written authorization identifying who is authorized to
receive the vehicle on your behalf.
