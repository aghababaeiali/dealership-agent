# Northgate Motors Service and Maintenance Policy

*Effective March 1, 2026*

## Included Services at Purchase

Every vehicle purchased from Northgate Motors includes:

- A complimentary multi-point inspection (brakes, tires, fluids, battery,
  belts and hoses, lights) performed within 7 days before delivery
- One free oil and filter change within the first **3 months or 3,000
  miles**, whichever comes first, using conventional or synthetic-blend
  oil matching manufacturer specification
- A full tank of fuel (or 90%+ charge for EVs) at delivery

## Service Department Hours and Rates

Our service department is open Monday-Friday 7:30am-6pm and Saturday
8am-2pm, closed Sunday. Our standard labor rate is **$129 per hour**,
billed in quarter-hour increments, for both warranty and out-of-pocket
repairs. Diagnostic fees ($89, waived if you proceed with the repair) may
apply for complex electrical or drivability issues.

## Recommended Maintenance Intervals

We follow manufacturer-recommended maintenance schedules, which generally
include:

- Oil and filter change every 5,000-7,500 miles (conventional) or
  7,500-10,000 miles (full synthetic), per your owner's manual
- Tire rotation every 5,000-7,500 miles
- Brake fluid flush every 2 years or 30,000 miles
- Cabin and engine air filter replacement every 15,000-20,000 miles
- Transmission fluid service per manufacturer schedule (varies widely by
  make - ranges from 30,000 to 100,000 miles for lifetime-fill
  transmissions)

Warranty coverage under our Warranty policy requires that you not miss
more than 2 consecutive scheduled maintenance intervals; we recommend
keeping receipts from any service performed outside our shop as proof of
compliance.

## Loaner Vehicles

If your vehicle requires a repair expected to take longer than 4 hours,
we offer a complimentary loaner vehicle (subject to availability) with 48
hours' advance notice for scheduled maintenance, or same-day for warranty
repairs when a loaner is on-site. A valid driver's license, proof of
insurance, and a $200 refundable hold on a credit card are required to
take a loaner.

## Recalls

We perform manufacturer safety recall repairs at no charge, regardless of
where the vehicle was purchased, as long as we are a franchised dealer for
that manufacturer. For makes we are not franchised to service, we will
direct you to the nearest franchise dealer and can often schedule the
appointment on your behalf.

## Bringing Your Own Parts

Our service department does not install customer-supplied parts for
warranty or scheduled maintenance work, due to liability and parts
traceability requirements. Exceptions may be made for out-of-pocket
cosmetic accessory installs (e.g., floor mats, cargo liners) at the
service manager's discretion.

## Appointments and Walk-Ins

We accept walk-ins for oil changes and tire rotations on a first-come,
first-served basis, with typical same-day turnaround of 60-90 minutes.
All other repairs require an appointment; same-week availability is
typical, though it can extend to 2 weeks during high-volume seasons
(spring and pre-holiday travel periods).

## Mobile and Off-Site Service

We do not currently offer mobile/at-home service. All repairs and
maintenance must be performed at our service center.
